FAQ

Common questions

Does it cost anything to register?

No. Registering as a Kasper vendor is free.

Do I need a UAE trade license?

Yes. We work with UAE-licensed companies, and need your trade license, TRN and VAT registration certificate to onboard you.

I only have a few machines or trucks. Can I still join?

Yes. Independent owners and small fleets are welcome. Tell us what you operate and where, and we'll match you to jobs that fit.

Who sets the price?

You share your rates with us during onboarding and for each job request. Kasper handles the quote to the client.

How long does approval take?

We aim to review documents within 2 working days, then call your authorized person to confirm the details.

I'm a client looking to book equipment or freight.

Head to kasperpro.com to send a request.
